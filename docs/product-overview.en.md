# Product overview

Ingot is a production-data and process-analysis system for manufacturing operations. It connects equipment processes, batches, workpieces, recipes, tooling, and inspections into a continuous production history, so engineers can move from an abnormal result to the matching batch, station, process stage, and original record.

## Product value

Manufacturing sites already generate large amounts of data, but that data is often scattered across equipment, instruments, spreadsheets, and business systems. Ingot links it around the production cycle so process and outcome can be reviewed, compared, and analyzed together.

With Ingot, teams can:

- review what happened during a batch;
- compare adjacent batches, machines on the same recipe, and similar production cycles;
- locate where a yield, dimension, cycle-time, or defect change appeared;
- drill from an analysis result into original curves, inspections, and production context;
- preserve expert investigation methods as reusable analysis plans.

## Product components

### Edge

Edge runs close to production and continuously collects equipment parameters, state changes, and process-stage data. It works with common plant data sources and resumes delivery of buffered records after connectivity returns.

### Platform

Platform stores production histories and process configuration. It provides views for equipment, workpieces, batches, cycles, inspections, recipes, tooling, and analysis. Teams maintain the business meaning needed for analysis and move from operating summaries to detailed records.

### Ingot Chat

Ingot Chat is the entry point for process investigations. Engineers describe a problem in everyday language, and the system confirms the data scope, retrieves records, aligns cycles, calculates metrics, and organizes the result. Metrics in the answer open the related production records.

## Who uses it

| Role | Main work |
|---|---|
| Process engineer | Compare cycles and review relationships between parameters and quality outcomes |
| Quality engineer | Review inspection distributions, defect concentration, and related production processes |
| Equipment engineer | Observe machine differences, cycle-time changes, and tooling-use trends |
| Production leader | Understand batch performance, impact scope, and investigation progress |
| Plant IT and implementation team | Connect plant data and maintain deployment and access scope |

## From data to conclusion

1. Plant data enters Edge with equipment, workpiece, batch, and production-cycle context.
2. Platform organizes parameters, states, and inspections from the same run into a production history.
3. An engineer starts from a batch, machine, workpiece, or question.
4. Ingot checks data quality, selects comparable cycles, calculates metrics, and compares differences.
5. The result appears as a summary, charts, and related records for the team to review and act on.
